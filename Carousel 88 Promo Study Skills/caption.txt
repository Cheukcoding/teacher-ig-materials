📌 Study skills for Business and Economics. Not general revision advice. Specific to how the Edexcel exam works.

📚 The session covers: how to read a source extract efficiently under time pressure, how to identify which spec point the question is testing, how to plan a 9 or 12-mark answer in under two minutes, and how to allocate time across the paper.

📈 Students who read the extract systematically before looking at the questions score higher on application marks. Students who plan their structure before writing score higher on analysis and evaluation.

⚠️ Study skills are most useful at the start of revision season, not the week before the exam. Building a reading and planning habit takes a few weeks to stick.

💾 One session, focused entirely on process, not content. Applicable to every question you sit afterwards.
