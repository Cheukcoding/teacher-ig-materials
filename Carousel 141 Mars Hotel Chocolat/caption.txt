📌 Mars acquired Hotel Chocolat. A global confectionery giant buying a premium UK chocolate brand.

📚 Hotel Chocolat built a direct-to-consumer model: its own stores, website and gifting business. Mars buys distribution reach that Hotel Chocolat could not afford to build alone. Hotel Chocolat buys manufacturing scale and working capital.

📈 GCSE lens (1.2 growing the business): acquisition gives the acquirer a brand, a customer base and a channel that would take years to build organically. A-Level lens (3.2 corporate strategy): horizontal integration between two confectionery businesses creates economies of scale and removes a competitor from the premium segment.

⚠️ The risk for Hotel Chocolat: mass distribution can dilute the premium positioning. If Mars sells Hotel Chocolat in supermarkets at a discount, the scarcity that justified the premium price disappears.

💾 Acquisition, brand dilution and positioning are three topics in one story. The extract will give you the data to decide which one the question is about.
