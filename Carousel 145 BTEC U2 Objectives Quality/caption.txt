📌 BTEC Unit 2. Learning Aim A: business objectives. Learning Aim B: business stakeholders and their influence.

📚 Unit 2 is a set assignment, not an exam. The brief is issued by Pearson and changes each year. Your answers must respond to the specific scenario in the brief, not to generic business theory.

📈 The most common Unit 2 gap: writing general theory without applying it to the scenario business. "SMART objectives are Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound" earns one mark. Using the brief's business context to explain why each SMART criterion matters for that business's specific goal earns the marks.

⚠️ Quality assurance in Unit 2: the assignment asks you to apply quality concepts to the scenario. Name the concept, explain what it means for this business's operations, and say what the consequence of poor quality would be for that specific business.

💾 BTEC marks come from application to the brief. Content knowledge alone is not enough.
