📌 GCSE 2025. Correct working, wrong answer: 0 marks. Right answer, almost no working: 2 marks.

📚 The question: break-even 3,000 units, price £7, variable cost £3. What are the fixed costs? The common answer was £60,000. The right answer is £12,000.

📈 Contribution per unit = £7 - £3 = £4. Fixed costs = 3,000 x £4 = £12,000. The £60,000 error comes from multiplying by total output, not break-even output.
