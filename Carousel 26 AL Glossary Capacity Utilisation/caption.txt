📌 Capacity utilisation. The calculation looks simple. The evaluation question around it is where the marks hide.

📚 Definition: the proportion of maximum output that a business is currently producing. Formula: (current output / maximum output) x 100. 100% means every unit of capacity is in use.

📈 High capacity utilisation reduces unit fixed costs. That lifts profit margin. But running at 100% leaves no room for new orders, breakdowns or demand spikes. Businesses typically target 80-90% as efficient without the risk.

⚠️ The exam asks whether a business should increase or reduce capacity. The right answer depends on demand forecasts and the cost of holding spare capacity versus the cost of turning orders away.

💾 Capacity utilisation is most useful in evaluate questions when the business faces a choice: expand, subcontract, or cut capacity.
