📌 DoorDash acquired Deliveroo. Two food delivery platforms, one merged business. The exam question this becomes is about market structure.

📚 Fewer platforms means less competition for the remaining one. That gives pricing power: higher fees to restaurants, higher delivery charges to customers, lower pay negotiating power with drivers.

📈 GCSE lens (1.2.4 competitive environment): fewer competitors in a market tend to push prices up and service standards down. A-Level lens (1.1.3 market structures): a merger between two large players moves the market from oligopoly towards monopoly.

⚠️ Competition authorities may block or impose conditions. The CMA reviews mergers where the combined share creates market power. A blocked merger is its own evaluate question.

💾 Every major merger is a market structure question. The extract will give you the shares. Use them.
