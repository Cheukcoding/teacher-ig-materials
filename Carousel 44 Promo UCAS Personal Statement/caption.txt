📌 UCAS personal statement. The section most students leave until too late.

📚 A Business or Economics personal statement needs to show that you have read beyond the syllabus and can connect real business events to the concepts you have studied. Admissions tutors read hundreds that list skills. Fewer show genuine curiosity.

📈 What I help with: structure (three parts: academic interest, wider reading, and why this course at this level), the specific examples that show engagement not just awareness, and the editing to cut everything that sounds generic.
