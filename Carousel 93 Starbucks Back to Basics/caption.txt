📌 Starbucks sold too many different drinks. So in 2025 it said it would cut around 30% of its menu.

📚 Custom orders got complicated, queues got longer, and sales fell. Brian Niccol, formerly of Chipotle, took over as chief executive in September 2024.

📈 Fewer drinks means faster orders. The target is about four minutes for most in-store orders, so each barista serves more people per hour. GCSE: 2.2.1 product and 2.3.1 operations. A-Level: 2.4.1 efficiency and 1.4.5 leadership.

⚠️ Coffee is easy to buy next door. Cutting 30% of the menu only works if the experience, ceramic mugs and seating included, keeps people coming back.
