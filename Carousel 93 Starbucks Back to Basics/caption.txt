📌 Starbucks went back to basics. Less menu complexity, more time for the barista to do one thing well.

📚 Starbucks had expanded its menu to hundreds of combinations. Service slowed, customer satisfaction fell and costs rose. The new strategy: cut the menu, reduce customisation, speed up service and bring back the writing-on-cups ritual.

📈 GCSE lens (2.2.4 product): a simpler product range reduces costs and speeds delivery but risks losing customers who valued the variety. A-Level lens (3.3 marketing strategy): operational complexity at scale destroys the customer experience that built the brand.

⚠️ Simplification requires a confidence that the core product is strong enough without the additions. If the core is weak, simplification just removes the things that were covering it.
