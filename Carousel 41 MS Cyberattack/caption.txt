📌 M&S shut down online orders after a cyberattack. Revenue stopped. The physical stores stayed open, but the online channel was down for weeks.

📚 Cyberattacks expose how dependent modern retail has become on digital infrastructure. Online is not just a channel, it is part of the fulfilment system, the stock management and the payment processing.

📈 GCSE lens (1.5.5 technology): technology is an opportunity and a risk. A business dependent on digital systems faces operational shutdown if those systems fail. A-Level lens (2.6.4 digital marketing and e-commerce): the competitive advantage of omnichannel retail comes with a single point of failure.

⚠️ The cost is not just lost sales. Reputational damage and the cost of remediation often exceed the immediate revenue loss.

💾 Business continuity planning is its own topic. The exam question is usually: how does this business recover, and what does it cost?
