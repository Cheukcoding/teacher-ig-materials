📌 Retained profit. The cheapest source of finance a business has, and the one most students forget to mention.

📚 Definition: the profit kept in the business after tax and dividends have been paid out to shareholders. It is reinvested into the business to fund growth, equipment or working capital.

📈 Why examiners like it: retained profit has no interest charge and no dilution of ownership. It is internal finance, so the owner stays in control. The trade-off is that it reduces the profit paid out to shareholders, which can reduce their return.

⚠️ A new business often has no retained profit. It cannot use this source of finance until it is profitable and has been for long enough to build a reserve.

💾 Sources of finance question: always check whether the business is new or established. That determines which sources are available.
