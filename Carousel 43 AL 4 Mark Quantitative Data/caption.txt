📌 A-Level 4-mark question with a table or calculation. The evidence is in the data. Most students ignore the actual numbers and write generic chains.

📚 The formula for a data-based 4-mark: state the trend or figure from the data (K), say what it means for this business using a second data point (P/A), extend the chain with a consequence (A).

📈 Example: "Revenue fell from £4.2m to £3.8m, a drop of £400,000 (K). This occurred despite a 3% price rise, which means volume fell by more than 3% (P/A). Therefore the product is price elastic, so the price rise worsened the revenue position rather than improving it (A)."

⚠️ Examiners want you to use the numbers, not just reference them. "Revenue fell" gives K. "Revenue fell from £4.2m to £3.8m, a fall of 9.5%" earns the marks.

💾 Quantitative questions: read the numbers, calculate the change, put the result inside a chain.
