📌 Motivation. Every theory is on the spec. The exam question is about which theory fits this specific business context.

📚 Key theories: Maslow's hierarchy of needs (five levels from survival to self-actualisation), Herzberg's two-factor theory (hygiene factors prevent dissatisfaction; motivators create satisfaction), Taylor's scientific management (financial incentives, specialised tasks).

📈 The exam does not ask you to recite the theory. It asks which theory best explains why this business's workers are or are not motivated, or which approach the business should use.

⚠️ The trap: listing all three theories and saying each is relevant. The examiner wants you to pick one, apply it to the extract, and justify the choice.

💾 Theory name plus one key idea plus link to the extract equals the application marks.
