📌 Product life cycle. Four stages, and the exam question is almost always about what happens at one specific stage.

📚 Introduction: high costs, low sales, marketing spend to build awareness. Growth: rising sales, new competitors enter. Maturity: sales peak, market saturated. Decline: sales fall, business must decide to extend or discontinue.

📈 Extension strategies at maturity or decline: new markets (export, new age groups), product modifications, rebranding, promotional campaigns, price reductions. Each one costs money and buys time.

⚠️ The exam trap: listing all four stages when the question asks only about one. Underline the stage named in the question before you write.

💾 The most tested stage is maturity or decline, because that is where the strategic decision sits.
