📌 AS Business. The extract is not optional reading. It is the source of your application marks.

📚 Every AS Business question comes with a source. The source contains the business context, the relevant data and the specific details the question expects you to use. Application marks (A marks) go to answers that use that source. General Business knowledge earns K only.

📈 The technique: before writing any paragraph, return to the source and find one piece of data or context that directly supports the point you are about to make. Write that detail into the paragraph.

⚠️ The most common error in AS Business: a student who knows the content well writes a technically correct answer that ignores the extract. The answer scores K, K, K across three paragraphs. No A marks.

💾 Rule: one extract fact per paragraph, minimum. Without it, the paragraph is general knowledge, not applied analysis.

👇 Save this. Extract use is the biggest grade-gap in every AS Business cohort.
