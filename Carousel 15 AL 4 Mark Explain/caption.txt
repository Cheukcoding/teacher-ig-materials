📌 A-Level 4-mark explain question. Most students write three times as much as they need to.

📚 Four marks, one chain. Knowledge: state the concept correctly. Application: link it to this business, using a detail from the extract. Analysis: extend the chain two steps, each one causing the next.

📈 The formula: "X because Y, which means Z, leading to W." You need one piece of evidence from the extract inside that chain. Without it, the chain scores K1 and A1. That is 2/4.

⚠️ Two marks in a 4-mark question come from application. Most students skip it because they think naming the business counts. It does not.

💾 One point. Two linked steps of development. One figure or fact from the extract. That is a 4/4 chain.
