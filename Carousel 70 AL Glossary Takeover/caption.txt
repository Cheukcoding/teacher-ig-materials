📌 Takeover. The word appears in mergers questions but the meaning matters more than the term.

📚 Definition: when one business acquires a majority stake in another without the target company's agreement. A hostile takeover is resisted by the target's management. A friendly acquisition is agreed by both boards.

📈 Reasons for takeover: access to new markets, elimination of a competitor, acquisition of assets, technology or talent, or achieving economies of scale quickly. The premium paid above the market price is the cost of gaining control.

⚠️ Takeovers often fail to deliver the expected benefits. Integration costs, cultural clashes and management distraction reduce the synergies that justified the price paid.

💾 In evaluation: the price paid tells you how confident the acquirer is. The post-merger results tell you whether that confidence was justified.

👇 Save this. Mergers and takeovers appear in growth and strategy questions every year.
