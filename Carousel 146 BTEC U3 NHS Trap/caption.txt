📌 BTEC Unit 3 synoptic exam. The NHS extract comes up, and students write about the NHS as if it is a private business.

📚 Unit 3 uses a case study business that could be a public sector organisation, a charity, a social enterprise or a private company. The objectives, stakeholders and success measures are completely different depending on the type.

📈 The trap: applying profit maximisation logic to an NHS trust. NHS trusts do not aim to maximise profit. They aim to meet patient need within budget, maintain quality standards and satisfy NHS England's performance targets. Applying the wrong objective framework gives an answer that looks right but answers the wrong question.

⚠️ Before writing any Unit 3 answer: identify the type of organisation in the case study. Public sector, private, charity or social enterprise each has its own primary objective. That objective changes every answer.

💾 Business type, then objectives, then analysis. In that order, every time.
