📌 12-mark assess using a liquidity question. The data is in the extract. The common error is ignoring it.

📚 A liquidity-based 12-marker gives you at least two years of ratio data and asks whether the business has a problem. A Level 3 answer uses both ratios, compares them across the years, and explains what the trend means for the business's ability to operate.

📈 Structure: Calculate current and acid test for both years. Identify the trend. Apply to the specific business context. Counter: is the ratio appropriate for this industry? Conclusion: is this a short-term or structural problem?

⚠️ The most common error is writing general facts about liquidity without using the given numbers. "A business with low liquidity may struggle to pay suppliers" earns K. "With the acid test falling from 1.3 to 0.8, this business cannot cover current liabilities without selling stock" earns A.

💾 Use the data. Then say what the data means for this business specifically.
