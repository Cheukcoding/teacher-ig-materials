📌 AS 8-mark assess question. Two distinct points. Most students write one strong point and then a variation of it.

📚 "Two distinct" means the second point cannot follow from the first. "Costs may rise" and "profits may fall as a result of rising costs" is one point, not two. "Costs may rise" and "brand reputation may be damaged" are two distinct points.

📈 Why it matters: examiners look for breadth of analysis on an 8-mark assess. One fully developed point with an undeveloped second scores less than two equally developed distinct points, each with three steps.

⚠️ Check before you write: can my second point exist if my first point did not happen? If no, they are the same point. Rewrite one of them.

💾 Two distinct points. Three steps each. No conclusion. That is the 8-mark assess structure.

👇 Save this. The "distinct" requirement is tested every paper.
