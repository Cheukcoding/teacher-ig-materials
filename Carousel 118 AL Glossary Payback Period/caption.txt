📌 Payback period. The simplest investment appraisal method and the most common calculation trap.

📚 Definition: the length of time it takes for an investment to generate enough net cash inflow to recover its initial cost. Formula: initial investment / annual net cash inflow. Used in months or years.

📈 The calculation assumes constant annual cash flows. If the flows are different each year, add them cumulatively until they equal the initial outlay, then calculate the remaining months.

⚠️ Payback period ignores profitability after the payback point. A project that pays back in two years and earns nothing afterwards scores identically to a project that pays back in two years and earns heavily for ten more. That is a built-in evaluation limitation.

💾 Payback is the first step. ARR is the second. IRR or NPV (if taught) is the third. Use all three together on a 12-mark investment appraisal question.
