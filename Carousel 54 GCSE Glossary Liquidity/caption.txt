📌 Liquidity. The difference between a business that is profitable and one that survives.

📚 Definition: the ability of a business to meet its short-term debts using its current assets. A liquid business can pay suppliers, wages and bills when they are due.

📈 Current ratio = current assets / current liabilities. A ratio above 1 means the business can cover its short-term debts. Below 1 means it cannot, even if it is making a profit.

⚠️ The exam trap: a profitable business with poor liquidity can still fail. If customers pay in 90 days but suppliers want payment in 30 days, the gap kills the business even when the profit is real.

💾 Profit is earned over time. Cash is needed today. Liquidity measures today.
