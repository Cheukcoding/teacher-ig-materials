📌 Magnum, Ben & Jerry's and Wall's. In March 2024 Unilever announced it no longer wanted any of them.

📚 The whole ice cream business was split off into its own listed company. About 7,500 jobs were cut in a productivity programme announced at the same time.

📈 Ice cream needs freezer transport and storage, and sales swing with the weather. Shampoo and soap don't. Running both made Unilever more complex, so it chose to get smaller and focus on beauty, personal care and home. GCSE: 2.1.2 changes in aims. A-Level: 3.1.2 corporate strategy and 3.2.4 reasons for staying small.

⚠️ Getting smaller has a cost. Around 7,500 jobs went in the same programme, a clear shareholder-versus-employee trade-off.
