📌 Unilever split off its ice cream division. Ben & Jerry's, Magnum, Walls. A £15bn separation from the main business.

📚 The logic of a demerger: if the market values a division less inside a conglomerate than it would as a standalone business, separating it unlocks that value. Ice cream has different seasonality, margins and growth rates from Unilever's consumer goods businesses.

📈 GCSE lens (1.2.2 business structure): a business can grow by acquisition or shrink by demerger. Demerger is a strategic choice, not a failure. A-Level lens (3.2 corporate strategy): portfolio management means keeping only the businesses where you have competitive advantage.

⚠️ The ice cream division is not being closed. It is becoming its own public company. Shareholders in Unilever will get shares in the new business. The value does not disappear, it reorganises.

💾 Demergers, spin-offs and portfolio restructuring are corporate strategy questions with a finance answer.
