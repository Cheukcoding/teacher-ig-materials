📌 How a Zoom lesson actually works. Most parents and students ask before they book.

📚 Before the lesson: send one marked answer or one question you want to work on. I read it and plan the session around the specific gap.

📈 In the lesson: we share screens on Zoom. I annotate your answer in real time. You see exactly where the mark would go on the Edexcel mark scheme. We rewrite one paragraph together, then you write the next one yourself while I watch.

⚠️ After the lesson: you leave with a written summary of the two or three things to fix, a model paragraph for the hardest part, and the next question to practise before the following session.

💾 All notes, booklets and model answers are sent by email and yours to keep. No equipment needed beyond a laptop and a stable connection.
