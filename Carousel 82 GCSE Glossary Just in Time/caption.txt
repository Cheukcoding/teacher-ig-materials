📌 Just in Time. One of those operations concepts that sounds straightforward but has two sides the exam always asks about.

📚 Definition: a stock management system in which materials and components are ordered and delivered only when they are needed for production, eliminating the cost of holding large stock levels.

📈 Benefits: no warehousing cost, no cash tied up in unsold stock, less waste from obsolete stock. Drawback: entirely dependent on reliable suppliers. One late delivery stops the production line. No buffer.

⚠️ JIT requires trust, long-term supplier relationships and good forecasting. A business that switches to JIT without those in place takes on more risk than it saves in cost.

💾 The exam question asks: is JIT right for this business? The answer depends on the supplier reliability, the nature of the product and how predictable demand is.
