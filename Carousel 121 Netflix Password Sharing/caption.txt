📌 Netflix cracked down on password sharing. Revenue went up. Subscribers went up too.

📚 The conventional view was that removing shared accounts would lose subscribers. Instead, many people who had shared accounts paid for their own. The demand that was hidden behind sharing became visible.

📈 GCSE lens (1.4.3 price / 2.2.3 pricing): a business that reduces access for non-paying users converts some of that use into paid revenue without losing the paying base. A-Level lens (3.3 marketing): the move from tolerance to enforcement is a pricing strategy change, not just a policy change.

⚠️ The crackdown worked for Netflix partly because the content library is strong enough that most users would not cancel. A weaker streaming service trying the same move would lose subscribers, not gain them.

💾 The lesson: strategy that works for a market leader may not work for a challenger. Context decides the outcome.
