📌 In 2023 Netflix started charging for password sharing outside the household. Subscriptions went up.

📚 Netflix estimated over 100 million households were watching on borrowed passwords. Charging them looked like a quick way to lose viewers.

📈 Many borrowers valued the shows enough to pay for their own account rather than stop watching. Paid memberships passed 300 million by the end of 2024. GCSE: 2.2.2 price. A-Level: 1.2.4 PED and 1.3.3 tiered pricing, with the extra member add-on and a cheaper plan with adverts.

⚠️ The cheaper plan with adverts helped too. Price-sensitive borrowers had a low-cost way to keep watching legally.
