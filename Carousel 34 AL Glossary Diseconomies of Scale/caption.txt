📌 Diseconomies of scale. The point where getting bigger starts costing more per unit.

📚 Definition: the increase in average costs that occurs when a business grows beyond its optimal size. Output rises but average cost per unit rises with it. The opposite of economies of scale.

📈 Main causes: communication breaks down as layers of management grow. Coordination costs rise. Workers feel less involved, motivation falls, and productivity drops. The business becomes harder to manage than to grow.

⚠️ Diseconomies are internal. External diseconomies (industry grows too fast, infrastructure can't keep up) are a separate concept and come up on longer questions.

💾 In evaluation: a business pursuing growth is not automatically better off. The point of diseconomy is where the strategy needs to change.

👇 Save this. Diseconomies appear in almost every growth question.
