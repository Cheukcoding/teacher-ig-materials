📌 Leadership styles. Four on the spec. The exam never just asks you to name them. It asks which one fits this situation.

📚 Autocratic: leader makes all decisions. Fast in a crisis. Poor for creativity. Democratic: team involved in decisions. Higher motivation. Slower. Laissez-faire: team self-manages. Best for expert, self-motivated workers. Paternalistic: leader decides but explains reasoning and considers workers' wellbeing.

📈 The evaluation: the best style depends on the task, the team, the time available and the stakes. An autocratic style in a deadline emergency makes sense. The same style with an expert creative team loses the best people.

⚠️ Examiners do not want you to say democratic is always best. That is a generic answer. The extract will give you context: the nature of the work, the team composition, the business situation. Use it.

💾 Leadership style question: name the style, say why it fits, say what would make a different style better. That is the evaluation.

👇 Save this. Leadership comes up in HRM and change management questions every year.
