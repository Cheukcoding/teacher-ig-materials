📌 Quality assurance. Not the same as quality control, and the exam expects you to know the difference.

📚 Quality control: checking for defects at the end of the production process. Problems are caught late, after resources have already been spent. Quality assurance: building quality into every stage of production. Problems are prevented, not just detected.

📈 Why the distinction matters: quality control produces waste (defective units already made). Quality assurance reduces waste (defective units never start). Most modern businesses use quality assurance systems over quality control because prevention is cheaper than rejection.

⚠️ The exam question sometimes asks whether a business should use TQM (Total Quality Management). TQM is a quality assurance approach that involves every employee in quality responsibility. The trade-off is training cost and time.

💾 Quality assurance is the system. Total Quality Management is one version of it. Know both.
