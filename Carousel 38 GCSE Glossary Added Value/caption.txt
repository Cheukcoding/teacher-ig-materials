📌 Added value. One of the most useful terms on the GCSE paper, and one of the most vaguely answered.

📚 Definition: the difference between the selling price of a product and the cost of the inputs used to make it. Added value = selling price - cost of bought-in materials.

📈 Why it matters: businesses increase added value through branding, design, quality and service, not just by cutting input costs. Apple adds value through design and the ecosystem. A bakery adds value by turning flour into a loaf worth more than the ingredients.

⚠️ Added value is not the same as profit. Profit deducts all costs including labour and overheads. Added value only deducts the direct material input costs.

💾 Higher added value means the business earns more from each unit without needing more customers. That is a growth strategy on its own.

👇 Save this. Added value comes up on nearly every marketing and finance question.
