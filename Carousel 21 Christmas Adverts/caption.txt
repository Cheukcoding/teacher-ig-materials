📌 John Lewis. Coca-Cola. Boots. Every November, the same brands spend millions on the same thirty seconds. Why?

📚 Christmas adverts are not about the product. They are about the brand. Emotional advertising builds long-run loyalty and price tolerance. A brand you feel something about is one you pay more for in January.

📈 GCSE lens (2.2 marketing): above-the-line advertising targets mass audiences to build brand image. A-Level lens (2.6.3): differentiation through brand values, not through price or features, creates sustainable competitive advantage.

⚠️ The cost is enormous and the return is indirect. That is a genuine evaluation point. Does short-run spend on emotion translate to long-run revenue? The data on brand recall says it often does.
