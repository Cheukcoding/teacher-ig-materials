📌 20-mark evaluate question. Four letters that change what you write: M, O, P, S.

📚 MOPS is the A-Level framework for structuring "it depends" evaluation. M: market conditions (competitors, state of the economy). O: objectives of the business (growth, profit, survival). P: position of the business (size, cash, brand). S: stakeholders affected (shareholders, employees, community).

📈 A Level 4 answer uses MOPS to show that the right decision changes depending on context. "Option A works if the business is in growth mode and has cash, but not if it is in survival mode with tight margins."

⚠️ MOPS is a thinking tool, not a template to copy. If you write all four regardless of the question, the examiner sees a structure with no judgement. Use the two that fit the extract.

💾 The conclusion must add a reason, not repeat the analysis. MOPS gives you that reason.
