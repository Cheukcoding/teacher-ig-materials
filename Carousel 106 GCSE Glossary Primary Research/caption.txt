📌 Primary research. One of two research methods on the spec, and the one with the clearest trade-off between quality and cost.

📚 Definition: research collected first-hand for a specific purpose. Methods include surveys, interviews, focus groups and observation. The data is original and directly relevant to the research question.

📈 Advantage: specifically tailored to the business's exact need. No existing data tells you what customers in this town think of this product, only primary research can. Disadvantage: expensive and time-consuming to collect well.

⚠️ The exam trap: students say primary research is always better because it is more relevant. Examiners expect the trade-off: for a small business with a limited budget, the cost of collecting primary research may outweigh the benefit of its specificity.

💾 The question is usually: which method is better for this business in this situation? Use the extract to decide.
