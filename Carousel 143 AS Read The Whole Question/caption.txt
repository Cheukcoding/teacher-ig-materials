📌 AS Business. The most common reason for a lost mark: answering the wrong part of the question.

📚 AS questions often have two parts in one sentence. "Explain one reason why the business might struggle to achieve its objective, given the information in the source." That question asks for: a reason, linked to the objective, using the source. Three requirements. Most students hit one or two.

📈 Before writing: underline the verb (explain, assess, justify), underline the focus (struggle to achieve), underline the constraint (given the information). Check the paragraph against all three before you move on.

⚠️ Writing a correct answer to a different question earns zero marks. GCSE 2025 proved it. A-Level papers do the same. The answer must fit the question exactly.

💾 Thirty seconds to read the question carefully saves five minutes of writing an answer that will not mark.

👇 Save this. It applies to every question on every paper.
