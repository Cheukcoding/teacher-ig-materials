📌 BTEC Unit 3 evaluate questions. The numbers in the case study are not decoration. They are the marks.

📚 A BTEC Unit 3 evaluate response that uses only qualitative arguments scores Level 2. Level 3 and 4 require quantitative evidence from the case study: revenue figures, cost ratios, growth percentages, financial ratios.

📈 The technique: before writing an evaluate response, read the case study data table. Identify two or three figures that directly support or challenge the decision being evaluated. Build those figures into the chain as evidence.

⚠️ The numbers do not have to be impressive. A 2% margin and a 45% gearing ratio are both useful evidence. The mark comes from using the number inside a cause-and-effect chain, not from the number being large or interesting.

💾 Every Level 4 evaluate response in BTEC Unit 3 contains at least two quantitative references to the case study. That is not a guideline. It is in the mark scheme.
