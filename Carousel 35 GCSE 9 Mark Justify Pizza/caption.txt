📌 Same question structure as every 9-mark Justify. Different business, same mistake.

📚 Justify means pick one option and defend it both ways. The student who writes: "Option A has these advantages, Option B has these advantages, so I choose A" is not justifying. That is just comparing.

📈 The 9/9 structure: pick Option A, give one reason for it, then argue against your own choice, then explain why you still pick A despite that. That counter-argument paragraph is where the Level 3 evaluation marks sit.

⚠️ For pizza businesses, the question will use context from the extract: a specific price point, a margin figure, a market trend. The justification must use those numbers, not generic pizza business facts.

💾 Justify: argue one side both ways, then decide. That decision with a reason is the last paragraph.
