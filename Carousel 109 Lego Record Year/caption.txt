📌 Lego posted record revenue. In a year when most toy companies struggled, Lego grew.

📚 Lego's strategy: target adults as well as children, license aggressively (Star Wars, Harry Potter, Marvel), and invest in retail experiences while keeping the physical product irreplaceable. The brand moved from "kids' toy" to "collectible creative product."

📈 GCSE lens (2.2.4 product / 2.2.3 pricing): extending a product range to new customer segments (adults) increases the addressable market without cannibalising the core product. A-Level lens (3.3 Ansoff Matrix): market development into the adult collector segment at a premium price point.

⚠️ Lego's success is partly structural: it cannot be pirated or copied cheaply because the precision tolerances are part of the product. That is a sustainable competitive advantage, not just a brand decision.

💾 Lego is one of the best case studies for brand extension and market development. Swipe for the spec connections.
