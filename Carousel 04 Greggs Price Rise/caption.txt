📌 Greggs put the Big Deal up from £5 to £5.25. Its profit that half-year was already up 22.9%.

📚 Costs were expected to rise about 3% in 2026: flour, energy, wages, packaging. The CEO has warned that if the Middle East conflict drags on, more of that will reach the customer.

📈 The detail most people skip is the hedge. Greggs fixed 90% of its 2026 energy costs, but only about 50% for 2027. That's 1.5.4 inflation at GCSE and price elasticity at A-Level. A 25p rise is only safe if customers still want a Greggs at £5.25.

⚠️ KFC, Subway and Wendy's are all expanding. If one of them holds its price, £5.25 starts to look expensive fast.
