📌 Another chain closed. Another empty unit. The high street is not dying, but it is changing shape.

📚 Footfall data shows people still visit town centres. They visit for experience: food, services, leisure. They buy products online. The businesses closing sell products. The ones opening sell experiences.

📈 GCSE lens (1.6 growth and decline): a business that cannot adapt to changing buying habits sees revenue fall even as the total market grows online. A-Level lens (2.6.3 marketing strategy): the channel of distribution is now part of the competitive advantage, not just the product.

⚠️ Not every closure is a failure of the business. Some are deliberate exits from unprofitable locations. The question is whether the brand survives in another form.
