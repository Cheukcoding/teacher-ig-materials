📌 Black Friday. Prices down 30%. Revenue still up. How?

📚 Discounting works when volume rises faster than margin falls. A 30% discount needs a significant jump in units sold just to break even on revenue. The businesses that profit are the ones managing stock, not just slashing prices.

📈 GCSE lens (2.2.3 pricing): promotional pricing attracts buyers, clears stock, and builds footfall. A-Level lens (1.3.3): penetration or psychological pricing? The original price was set high enough to make the deal look bigger.

⚠️ Many "Black Friday" deals use artificially inflated original prices. This is price anchoring. The Competition and Markets Authority has investigated retailers for it.

💾 Price is not just a number. It signals value, triggers behaviour, and sits inside a strategy.
