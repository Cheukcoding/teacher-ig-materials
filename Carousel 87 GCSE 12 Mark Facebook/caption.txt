📌 12-mark evaluate using Facebook. The extract decides what you write. Your prior knowledge of Facebook is a starting point, not the answer.

📚 The Facebook extract on a GCSE paper will give you a specific business decision: a price change, a market entry, a stakeholder conflict. The 12-mark answer addresses that decision, not Facebook's entire history.

📈 Structure: two sides of the specific question, three development steps each. Conclusion: which side matters more for this business right now, with one new reason from the context.

⚠️ The most common error: writing what you know about Facebook from outside the extract. The examiner gives you an extract because those are the facts you should use. General knowledge earns K. Extract-based chains earn A and E.

💾 Read the question. Read the extract. Underline the three most useful facts. Build the chain from those.
