📌 Klarna listed on the stock market. Buy now, pay later, then go public.

📚 Klarna's IPO raised capital and gave early investors a way to exit. The valuation at IPO reflected expectations of future profit, not current profit. Klarna was still loss-making at the time of listing, as most growth-stage fintech companies are.

📈 GCSE lens (1.2 business ownership): a private business becomes a PLC to raise capital from the public and to allow shareholders to sell shares. A-Level lens (3.2 finance): an IPO is a source of equity finance that does not require repayment, but gives shareholders a claim on future profits and dividends.

⚠️ The risk for Klarna: public markets are impatient. Quarterly results matter. If growth slows before profitability arrives, the share price falls and the raised capital is harder to use effectively.

💾 IPOs are a finance question and a business ownership question in one. They appear regularly on both GCSE and A-Level papers.
