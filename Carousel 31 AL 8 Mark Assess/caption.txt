📌 A-Level 8-mark assess question. Two sides. No conclusion. Most students write a conclusion anyway and lose the last two minutes they needed for depth.

📚 "Assess" at A-Level means weigh the evidence and reach a verdict within the analysis. It is not a 20-mark evaluate. It does not need MOPS. It needs two developed points, one on each side, each with three linked steps.

📈 Structure: Point one, because, leading to, therefore (three steps). Point two counter (because, leading to, therefore). The examiner uses both points to decide your level. Neither needs to win.

⚠️ The most common 8-mark error: writing three points at one step each. That gives you breadth at Level 2, not depth at Level 3. Fewer points, more development.

💾 8 marks, about 10 minutes. Two points. Three steps each. No conclusion line needed.

👇 Save this. The 8-mark structure is tested every paper.
