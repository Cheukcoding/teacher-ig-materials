📌 Porter's Generic Strategies. Three choices, and the exam always asks which one this business has chosen and why it works.

📚 Cost leadership: be the cheapest producer in the market. Differentiation: charge a premium by offering something competitors cannot easily copy. Focus: serve a narrow segment, either on cost or differentiation.

📈 The trap: students say a business "uses differentiation" without explaining what makes the differentiation sustainable. Porter's point was that differentiation must be hard to copy, otherwise competitors erode the premium.

⚠️ A business cannot do all three. Porter argued that "stuck in the middle" (neither cheapest nor differentiated) is the worst position because it offers no reason to buy from you specifically.

💾 In evaluation: which strategy is this business best positioned to sustain, given the extract's data on costs, brand and customer loyalty?
