📌 Contribution. The number that appears in break-even, margin of safety and product mix questions. Get the definition wrong and the whole calculation goes with it.

📚 Definition: selling price minus variable cost per unit. Contribution per unit is what each sale adds towards paying the fixed costs, and then to profit once fixed costs are covered.

📈 Total contribution = contribution per unit x number of units. Profit = total contribution - fixed costs. The order matters: contribution first, then profit. Many students subtract fixed costs from revenue directly, which gives profit but skips the contribution step the examiner wants.

⚠️ Contribution is most useful in multi-product businesses. The product with the highest contribution per unit is not always the best one to focus on if it takes longer to make or uses more capacity.

💾 Contribution per unit. Total contribution. Profit. Three calculations, one chain.
