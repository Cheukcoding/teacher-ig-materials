📌 Organisational structure. The diagram is easy. The exam question asks about what the structure means for communication, motivation and decision-making.

📚 Tall structure: many layers, narrow spans of control. Decisions move slowly. Managers supervise fewer people closely. Flat structure: fewer layers, wide spans of control. Decisions move faster. Managers supervise more people less closely.

📈 The spec link: span of control, chain of command, delegation, centralisation and decentralisation all connect to structure. A question about structure is usually also a question about one of these.

⚠️ A tall structure is not better or worse than a flat one. Each suits different businesses. A tall structure suits a business where tasks are complex and supervision is important. A flat structure suits a business where creativity and speed matter more.

💾 The exam question names a business and asks which structure suits it. Use the extract to decide.
