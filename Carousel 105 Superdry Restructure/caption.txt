📌 Superdry went into administration. The brand that made Japanese-inspired streetwear mainstream in the UK, restructured.

📚 Superdry expanded fast, licensed the brand widely, and lost control of the positioning. When fast fashion and premium brands both undercut it, the middle-market proposition collapsed. Sales fell, costs stayed high.

📈 GCSE lens (1.2 growing the business): growth through licensing reduces control over brand quality. If the licensed product looks cheaper, the core brand takes the damage. A-Level lens (2.6.3 marketing strategy): a brand that tries to be accessible and premium at the same time usually ends up being neither.

⚠️ The founder returned to lead the turnaround. Founder-led turnarounds often require the original brand values to be reasserted. That is a strategy question, not just a finance one.

💾 Superdry is a brand management and strategy case study. The extract will tell you which part the question is about.
