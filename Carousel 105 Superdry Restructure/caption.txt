📌 In 2024 Superdry's co-founder Julian Dunkerton bought his company back, and asked landlords to cut the rent.

📚 Shoppers had moved away from the heavily branded look, and sales fell. A court approved a restructuring plan, many landlords accepted lower rents, and Superdry left the London Stock Exchange.

📈 Rent stays the same whatever the sales. As revenue fell, fixed costs ate a bigger share of every pound, so cutting rent became the key to survival. GCSE: 1.3.2 costs and profit. A-Level: 2.3.3 business failure and 1.5.4 forms of business.

⚠️ Going private gives the founder control, but it also means less access to share capital.
