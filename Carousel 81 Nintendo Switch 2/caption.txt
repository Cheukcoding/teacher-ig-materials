📌 Nintendo Switch 2 launched. Fastest-selling console in Nintendo's history.

📚 Past 23 million units. Nintendo balanced a higher price point against demand that outran supply in the first months. A premium launch price on a product with strong brand loyalty and few substitutes: that is inelastic demand working as intended.

📈 GCSE lens (1.4.3 price / 1.2.4 competition): setting a high launch price works when demand is strong and rivals cannot offer a comparable substitute quickly. A-Level lens (1.3.3 pricing strategy): price skimming at launch recovers R&D costs early, before competition increases.

⚠️ Price skimming requires inelastic demand at launch. If the competitor response is fast, the window for the skim closes. Nintendo has managed this repeatedly by building platform lock-in through its game library.
