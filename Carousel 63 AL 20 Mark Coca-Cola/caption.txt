📌 20-mark evaluate using Coca-Cola. The biggest marks question on the paper. The most structured answer wins, not the longest.

📚 Level 4 (17-20 marks) needs: developed analysis on both sides of the argument, evidence from the extract, a conclusion that adds a reason beyond what the analysis already said, and a judgement that shows the two sides genuinely weigh against each other.

📈 Coca-Cola extract usually contains revenue data, market share, brand value or a specific strategic decision. The 20-mark question is about the strategic decision, not about Coca-Cola generally.

⚠️ The most common error: writing everything you know about Coca-Cola. Examiners want analysis of what the extract says, not a brand history.

💾 Structure: two sides, three steps each. MOPS conclusion. New reason at the end. That is a Level 4 answer.
