📌 GCSE 3-mark explain question. One point, two linked steps. Most scripts only write one.

📚 Structure: Point (because), leading to, therefore. The "leading to" is the step most students skip. Without it, you get 1/3 for knowledge alone.

📈 Example: "One benefit is that the business lowers its costs. This is because bulk buying reduces the price paid per unit, leading to a higher profit margin on each item sold, therefore the business can reinvest more into growth." Three marks: K, A, A.

⚠️ 3-mark questions are fast. Under four minutes. Don't add a fourth step or a conclusion. That wastes time and earns nothing extra.

💾 Three-mark questions are the easiest to bank if the structure is right. Most students write too much or too little.

👇 Save this. Practise one 3-marker every day until it feels automatic.
