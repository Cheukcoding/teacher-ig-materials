📌 9-mark Justify using Ocado. The structure is the same as every other Justify question. The context changes. The method does not.

📚 Pick one option from the question. Write one reason for it. Write one reason against it (your own choice). Write a final sentence explaining why you still pick it despite the counter.

📈 The Ocado extract will give you specific details: a revenue figure, a market position, a customer loyalty metric. Your paragraphs must use those details. "Ocado could expand" without the extract numbers scores Level 1.

⚠️ The counter-argument does not have to destroy your case. It just has to be real and developed. "Although expanding into new markets is risky given the high delivery costs in the extract, the 13.3% revenue growth shows demand is strong enough to absorb some of that cost."

💾 Justify: pick, argue for, argue against, decide. All four moves, not three.
