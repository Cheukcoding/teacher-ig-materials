📌 20-mark recommendation question. This is a specific variant the Edexcel A-Level uses. The structure is slightly different from a standard evaluate.

📚 A recommendation question asks you to argue for one course of action and justify it. You still need both sides, but the conclusion is a directive: "I recommend X because, in this context, the evidence from the extract shows that Y outweighs Z."

📈 The difference from standard evaluate: the conclusion commits. It does not sit in "it depends" territory. It makes a call and gives the reason for the call using the extract.

⚠️ Students who hedge the conclusion on a recommendation question miss Level 4. The examiner wants a supported judgement, not a balanced summary.

💾 Recommendation: argue both sides, then commit to one with a reason from the extract. The commitment is the mark.

👇 Save this. Recommendation questions appear on Paper 2 and 3 of A-Level Business.
