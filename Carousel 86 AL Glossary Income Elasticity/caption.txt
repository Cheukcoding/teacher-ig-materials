📌 Income elasticity of demand. The one elasticity that tells you which direction demand moves when the economy changes.

📚 Formula: % change in quantity demanded / % change in income. Positive YED means demand rises with income (normal good). Negative YED means demand falls as income rises (inferior good).

📈 The A-Level application: during a recession, income falls. Demand for inferior goods (budget supermarkets, own-brand products) rises. Demand for luxury goods falls. The business's YED value tells you which category it is in.

⚠️ The trap: a positive YED does not always mean the good is desirable. It just means demand moves with income. A luxury good has a positive YED greater than 1. A necessity has a positive YED between 0 and 1.

💾 YED above 1: luxury. YED between 0 and 1: necessity. YED below 0: inferior. Know all three for the 12 and 20-mark questions.
