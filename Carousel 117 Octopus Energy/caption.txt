📌 Octopus Energy grew from a startup to the UK's second-largest energy supplier in under a decade.

📚 Octopus built its own tech platform (Kraken) instead of using legacy systems. That made it cheaper to run and faster to scale. It signed up customers when rivals were going bankrupt during the 2021-22 energy crisis, and bought some of those failed businesses to grow further.

📈 GCSE lens (1.5.5 technology): investing in technology infrastructure creates long-run cost advantages. A-Level lens (3.4 business objectives): growth through market disruption, not just market development, using technology as the differentiator.

⚠️ Regulated markets (energy, water) create a floor on competition. Octopus grew because the regulator allowed it to, and because rivals failed to invest in technology before the market tested them.

💾 Octopus is a case study in technology-led growth and market disruption. Swipe for the spec connections.
