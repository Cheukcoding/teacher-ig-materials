📌 True story: a student wrote "Johnsons" in a 20-mark answer. Lost half the marks for it. Why? Naming the business isn't the same as using it.

📚 The missing word: share.

📈 Here's how it works with Greggs. Sales up 6.8% to £2.2bn last year, 121 new shops opened, now at 2,773 sites (aiming for 3,500). More share = buying flour & packaging in bulk = cheaper than what Pret can do with their £5.25 Big Deal.

⚠️ But careful. "Sales up 6.8%" ≠ "share up 6.8%". If rivals grew even faster, Greggs' share could've actually dropped.

💾 One word, worth marks on literally any question.
