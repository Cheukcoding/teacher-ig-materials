📌 A-Level 2025. One script used the new price as the denominator. 0/4. No method mark.

📚 PED = % change in quantity demanded / % change in price. % change = (new - original) / original x 100. Original always goes on the bottom. New figure on the bottom: zero marks, however neat the rest of the working.

📈 Same brewery, same 8% price rise. Cask ale PED -0.5: revenue up 3.7%. Canned range PED -1.8: revenue down 7.6%. Same price rise, opposite outcomes depending on how many substitutes the buyer sees on the shelf.

⚠️ A number with no interpretation earns little. The marks come from saying "inelastic, so a price rise increases revenue" not just writing -0.5.

💾 The calculation is one step. The conclusion about revenue is where the marks actually sit.
