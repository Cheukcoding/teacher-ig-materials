📌 6-mark question using Ocado. Two points on the business in the extract. Not on online retail generally.

📚 Ocado is the exam's favourite online grocer. The question will give you revenue data, a market position or a technology detail. Your two points must use those details, not your prior knowledge of Ocado.

📈 Structure for each paragraph: state the point relevant to Ocado, say why it matters using one figure from the extract, extend to a business consequence, link to the specific question focus.

⚠️ The six-mark question does not need a conclusion. Two paragraphs, three steps each. Anything beyond that earns nothing and wastes time on the 9 or 12-mark question that follows.

💾 "Discuss" on GCSE means: two points, developed. "Discuss" on A-Level means: both sides with analysis. Know which paper you are on.
