📌 Liquidity ratios. The calculation is four numbers. The interpretation is the actual mark.

📚 Current ratio = current assets / current liabilities. Ideal range: 1.5 to 2. Acid test ratio = (current assets - inventories) / current liabilities. Ideal range: 1 to 1.5. Below 1 on the acid test means the business cannot meet short-term debts without selling stock.

📈 In evaluation: a current ratio of 2.5 means the business has excess cash sitting idle. Too high is also a problem. The optimal ratio depends on the industry: retailers hold more stock and have lower ratios; service businesses hold less stock and can maintain higher ones.

⚠️ Ratios mean nothing in isolation. The examiner expects a trend (two years of data) or a comparison (industry benchmark). One number is K. Two numbers in comparison is A.

💾 Calculate both ratios. Compare. Say what the comparison means for this business's ability to pay.
