📌 12-mark evaluate question. The marks are not where most students look for them.

📚 GCSE 12-mark: two sides and a conclusion. Level 3 (9-12 marks) needs developed points on both sides and a supported judgement at the end. The conclusion cannot just repeat what you already wrote.

📈 The conclusion earns marks by adding a new reason for the decision. "Option A is better because, in this context, X outweighs Y since the business is small and has limited cash." That is a judgement. "Overall I think option A is best" is a summary.

⚠️ Two-thirds of the marks sit in analysis and evaluation. Students who write four points with no development score lower than students who write two points with three linked steps each.

💾 12-mark structure: Point, because, leading to, therefore. Counter-argument. Conclusion with a new reason.
