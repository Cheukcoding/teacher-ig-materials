📌 Price elasticity of demand. Four ways to lose marks in one question.

📚 PED = % change in quantity demanded / % change in price. Elastic demand: PED below -1. A price rise reduces total revenue. Inelastic demand: PED between 0 and -1. A price rise increases total revenue.

📈 The revenue rule is the one the examiner tests most: elastic plus price rise = less revenue. Inelastic plus price rise = more revenue. That link must be explicit in your chain, not assumed.

⚠️ Four traps: wrong denominator (new price instead of original), no negative sign, interpreting -0.5 as elastic, and calculating PED without saying what it means for revenue. Each one costs marks independently.

💾 Calculate. Interpret the sign. State elastic or inelastic. Say what it means for revenue. Four steps, every time.
