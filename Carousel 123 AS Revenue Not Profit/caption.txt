📌 AS Business. Revenue and profit are not the same. That sentence earns marks every year.

📚 Revenue = price x quantity. Profit = revenue - total costs. A business can have high revenue and low profit if costs are also high. A business can have low revenue and high profit if costs are very low.

📈 The calculation chain: revenue is the starting point. Deduct cost of goods sold to get gross profit. Deduct overheads to get net (operating) profit. Each step is tested separately on the paper.

⚠️ The common confusion: a student writes "revenue increased by 10%, so the business is more profitable." That is only true if costs did not rise by the same amount or more. Revenue going up while costs go up faster means less profit, not more.

💾 Always check: did costs change? Revenue without costs is not a profit statement.

👇 Save this. Revenue versus profit is tested on almost every AS paper.
