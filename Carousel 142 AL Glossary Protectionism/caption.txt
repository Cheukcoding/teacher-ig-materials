📌 Protectionism. The external factor that appears in global business questions and that most students only half-understand.

📚 Definition: government policies designed to restrict imports and protect domestic industries. Methods: tariffs (tax on imports), quotas (limits on import quantity), subsidies (payments to domestic producers), embargoes (bans on specific countries' goods).

📈 Why governments use it: to protect jobs, infant industries, strategic sectors (steel, energy) or to retaliate against trading partners who did the same. Why economists criticise it: it raises prices for consumers and reduces the pressure on domestic businesses to improve.

⚠️ The exam evaluation: protectionism protects the short-run. In the long-run, protected industries may become less competitive than they would have been without protection. The business in the extract determines which side the question sits on.

💾 Protectionism is a government influence topic that appears in global business and economic environment questions.
