📌 Ansoff Matrix. Four strategies, and the exam always asks which one fits this business and why.

📚 Market penetration: existing product, existing market. Lowest risk. Market development: existing product, new market. Product development: new product, existing market. Diversification: new product, new market. Highest risk.

📈 The matrix is a risk framework as much as a strategy tool. Moving from existing to new in either dimension increases risk. Moving in both dimensions at once (diversification) combines both risks.

⚠️ The exam does not ask you to draw the matrix. It asks which strategy the business is using, or which it should use given the extract data. Name the strategy, say which quadrant, then use the extract to support the choice.

💾 Diversification sounds ambitious. In evaluation, it is the riskiest option unless the business has strong cash reserves and management capacity.
