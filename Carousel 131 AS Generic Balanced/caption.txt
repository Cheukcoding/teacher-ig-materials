📌 AS Business. A "balanced" answer is not the same as a "generic" answer. That distinction costs marks.

📚 Balanced means one side for and one side against, both developed. Generic means neither side is linked to the specific business in the question. A generic balanced answer scores Level 2. A balanced answer with extract-specific application scores Level 3.

📈 The check: read your two paragraphs. Could they be about any business in any sector? If yes, they are generic. Rewrite each paragraph to include at least one detail from the question stem or extract.

⚠️ "On one hand, the business could benefit from economies of scale" is generic. "On one hand, with output at 12,000 units against a capacity of 20,000, Riverside Kitchens has room to expand and spread fixed costs further" is specific. Same concept. Very different mark.

💾 Generic balanced = Level 2. Specific balanced = Level 3. The extract is the tool.

👇 Save this. Specificity is the most consistent gap in AS Business answers.
