📌 2026 GCSE. The Justify structure has not changed. The marks are still in the same place.

📚 Justify = pick one option and argue it both ways. Not both options. Not a comparison. One option. One reason for it. One reason against it. One conclusion explaining why you still pick it.

📈 The last paragraph is where Level 3 marks sit. It cannot just repeat the points above. It needs a new reason: "I choose option A because even accounting for the risk in paragraph two, the margin gain from the extract outweighs it in the short run."

⚠️ The most common 2025 error was arguing two positives with no counter. That scores Level 2 however well written. The counter-argument to your own choice is not optional.

💾 Justify: argue for, argue against, decide. Three paragraphs. One chain in each.
