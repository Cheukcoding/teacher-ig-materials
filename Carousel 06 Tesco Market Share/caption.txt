📌 Tesco's sales went up. Its market share went down. Both are true at the same time.

📚 The grocery market grew about 2%, mostly from inflation. Tesco grew 1.7%. M&S grew 14.8%, Lidl 8.0%, Ocado 13.3%. When rivals grow faster, your slice shrinks even if your own sales rise.

📈 GCSE lens (1.2.4): more competitors growing faster means the market leader loses ground without losing revenue. A-Level lens (1.3.3): Tesco under-inflated, raising prices more slowly than rivals, to protect volume over margin.

⚠️ Tesco still holds roughly double Sainsbury's share. The direction of change matters less when the gap is that wide.
