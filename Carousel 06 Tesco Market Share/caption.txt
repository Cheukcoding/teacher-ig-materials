📌 Tesco's sales went up 1.7% this summer. Its market share went down anyway, for the fourth report in a row.

📚 28.5% in February. 27.9% by July. Busier tills, smaller slice. Most students see "sales up" in the extract and stop reading right there.

📈 The answer is in the rivals. M&S grew 14.8%, Ocado 13.3%, Lidl 8.0%. Tesco grew, just slower than everyone chasing it. That's 1.2.4 competition at GCSE and 1.1.1 market share at A-Level.

⚠️ Don't jump to "Tesco is in trouble". 27.9% is still nearly double Sainsbury's 15.2%. It's sliding from a very big lead.
