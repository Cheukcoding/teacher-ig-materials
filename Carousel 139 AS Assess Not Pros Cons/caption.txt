📌 AS Business 8-mark assess question. It is not a pros-and-cons list. That is the most common reason for a Level 2 score.

📚 Pros and cons: two lists of bullet points, each item one sentence. No development. No cause-and-effect chain. No application. Level 2 at best.

📈 Assess with developed analysis: each side is a paragraph. Point, because, leading to, therefore. Three linked steps minimum. Each step causes the next. One piece of extract context in each paragraph.

⚠️ A student who writes four bullet points on each side and reaches Level 2 with good content would score Level 3 with the same content and half the points if each one was developed into a full chain.

💾 The content is already there. The development is the gap. Fewer points, more steps.
