📌 Productivity. The metric that connects labour cost to output without changing either number individually.

📚 Definition: the output per unit of input, usually measured as output per worker or per hour. Labour productivity = total output / number of workers.

📈 Why it matters: a business that raises productivity produces more without hiring more. That reduces average cost per unit and improves competitiveness. The exam question usually asks how a specific business could improve productivity given the context.

⚠️ Productivity and output are not the same. Output can rise because more workers were hired. Productivity only rises when each worker produces more. The formula keeps them separate.

💾 In evaluation: technology raises productivity but costs capital. Training raises productivity but takes time. The right answer depends on the business's cash position and the urgency of the improvement.
