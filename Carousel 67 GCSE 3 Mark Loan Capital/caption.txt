📌 3-mark question on loan capital. One point, two steps. Know which direction the chain goes.

📚 Loan capital is borrowed money repaid over time with interest. The chain for a benefit: "One advantage is that loan capital provides a large sum up front. This is because the business receives all the funds immediately, leading to the ability to finance capital investment that retained profit could not cover."

📈 The chain for a drawback: "One disadvantage is that the business must pay interest on the loan. This increases costs each month, leading to a lower net profit margin, even in periods when revenue falls."

⚠️ Many students write both an advantage and a disadvantage for a 3-mark question. The question only asks for one. Two shorter chains each worth 1-2 marks score less than one full chain worth 3.

💾 Direction matters. Choose: benefit or drawback. Then build the full chain.
