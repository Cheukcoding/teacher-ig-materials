📌 Heathrow shut for hours after a power cut. 200,000 passengers. Hundreds of flights cancelled.

📚 A single substation failure closed Europe's busiest airport. The infrastructure was old. The backup systems were not enough. Every airline and cargo business that used Heathrow that day bore the cost.

📈 GCSE lens (1.5.5 technology): infrastructure failure is an operational risk every business depends on others to manage. A-Level lens (2.4.2 operations): supply chain and transport disruption creates costs that cannot be recovered in the short run.

⚠️ Heathrow is a natural monopoly with significant stakeholders: airlines, freight businesses, passengers, surface transport, local employment. Disruption here ripples wide and fast.

💾 Infrastructure risk is external, not internal. But the exam question will ask how the business responds. That is your evaluate frame.
