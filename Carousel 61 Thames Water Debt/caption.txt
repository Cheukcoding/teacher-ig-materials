📌 Thames Water supplies 16 million customers across London and the Thames Valley. It also carries around £19bn of debt.

📚 In 2025 the High Court approved a £3bn emergency loan just to stop it running out of cash. Meanwhile Ofwat let bills rise 35% over five years.

📈 Interest on that debt eats cash that could fix pipes. More leaks, angrier regulator, nervous investors, and customers end up paying. GCSE: 1.5.1 stakeholders and 1.3.4 sources of finance. A-Level: 3.5.2 gearing and 2.3.2 liquidity.

⚠️ Those 16 million customers can't switch supplier. That's why Ofwat sets the price, and why the state can't simply let Thames Water stop.
