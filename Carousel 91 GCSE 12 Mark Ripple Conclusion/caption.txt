📌 12-mark question. Most students write a conclusion. Most of those conclusions are a summary. The examiner wants something different.

📚 A GCSE 12-mark conclusion must add a reason, not repeat what the paragraphs already said. The examiner calls this the "ripple" conclusion: the final paragraph explains which factor matters most in this specific context, and why that outweighs the other side.

📈 Example conclusion: "Overall, raising prices is the better option because, as the extract shows, the business has a loyal customer base and few direct competitors, so the risk of losing customers to substitutes is lower than the cost of not raising prices to cover the margin squeeze."

⚠️ "Overall, I believe X is the best option" without a new reason is a summary, not a conclusion. It earns no evaluation marks beyond what the analysis already gave.

💾 One extra sentence, one new reason, linked to the extract. That is the conclusion.
