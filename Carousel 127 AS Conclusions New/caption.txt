📌 AS Business. The conclusion on an 8-mark assess question must add a new reason. Most conclusions repeat the analysis.

📚 A conclusion that says "overall, I think X is the better strategy because it benefits the business more" is a summary. It repeats what the two paragraphs already showed. No new marks.

📈 A conclusion that earns marks: "Overall, X is the better strategy in this case because, given the business's limited cash reserves shown in the extract, the lower upfront cost of X means the business can remain liquid while investing, unlike Y which requires capital the business does not have."

⚠️ The new reason must come from the extract. It cannot be invented. The extract gives you the constraint or the context that makes one option genuinely better than the other right now.

💾 Conclusion rule: one new reason, linked to the extract, that tips the balance. One sentence is enough.

👇 Save this. Conclusions on 8-mark and above questions are consistently the gap.
