📌 Oxbridge interview prep for Economics and Business. The questions are harder than any paper you have sat.

📚 Oxbridge interviews test whether you can apply what you know to a problem you have never seen. You are not expected to know the answer. You are expected to think aloud, make reasonable assumptions, and revise your position when challenged.

📈 What we practise: a short unfamiliar case study, three minutes to think, then a structured response. Push and counter from me. Recovery when the first answer is wrong.

⚠️ The biggest mistake: revising content and hoping the interview tests what you know. Interviews test how you think. Content helps, but the thinking process is what gets you the offer.
