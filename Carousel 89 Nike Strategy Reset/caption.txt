📌 Nike pulled back from its direct-to-consumer push and returned to its wholesale partners. A public reversal of strategy.

📚 Nike spent years cutting out retailers like Foot Locker to sell direct. Revenue from its own channels grew, but wholesale accounts were lost and market share slipped. The reset restores relationships that took decades to build.

📈 GCSE lens (2.2 marketing / 2.6.3 channels of distribution): removing the retailer increases margin per unit but reduces reach and sales volume. A-Level lens (3.3 marketing strategy): a distribution strategy that reduces availability hurts brand penetration, even for a dominant brand.

⚠️ Nike's DTC strategy worked during peak brand loyalty periods. It struggled when competitors kept their retail presence and maintained shelf space visibility. Visibility in stores drives impulse purchases that online search does not.

💾 Channel strategy is not just about profit per unit. It is about reach and brand presence. That is your evaluation.
