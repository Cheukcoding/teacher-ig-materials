📌 For years Nike pulled back from shops like JD Sports and Foot Locker to sell direct through its own app and stores. In 2024 it started going back.

📚 Sales slowed, the share price fell sharply, and around 1,600 jobs went in early 2024. In October, Elliott Hill replaced John Donahoe as chief executive.

📈 Nike gave up shelf space to earn more per sale. On and Hoka moved into that space, and shoppers trying trainers on in store started picking them instead. GCSE: 2.2.4 place. A-Level: 1.3.4 distribution and 3.1.2 corporate strategy, margin traded against reach.

⚠️ Every direct sale still earned Nike a higher margin. Going back to JD Sports and Foot Locker means giving some of that up.
