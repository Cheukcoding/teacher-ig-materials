📌 AS Business. The question stem contains the context you need. Most students read it once and forget it.

📚 Every AS Business question comes with a source extract and a question stem. The stem names a specific business, gives a specific context, and sometimes gives data. All of that is available for application marks.

📈 The technique: before writing, underline one or two pieces of data or context from the stem. Build one of those into each paragraph. Without it, the answer is generic. With it, the answer earns application marks.

⚠️ Context in the stem is not decoration. Examiners put it there because it is needed for the A marks. Answers that ignore the stem and write general Business knowledge score K and A1 at best.

💾 Read the stem. Underline two facts. Use both. That is the application mark.

👇 Save this. Extract use is the most consistent weakness in AS Business answers.
