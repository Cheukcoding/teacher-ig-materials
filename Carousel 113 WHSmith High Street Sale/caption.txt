📌 WHSmith sold its high street stores to focus entirely on travel retail. Airports, train stations, hospitals.

📚 High street WHSmith: low footfall growth, margin pressure, no competitive advantage against supermarkets or online. Travel WHSmith: captive audience, higher willingness to pay, no direct competitor at the gate.

📈 GCSE lens (1.2 growing the business): a business can shrink in one market and grow in another as a deliberate strategy. Selling a division is not failure; it can free capital for the more profitable part. A-Level lens (3.2 portfolio management): divesting low-return assets to concentrate resources where the margin is highest.

⚠️ The travel retail model depends on passenger numbers. A disruption to travel (a pandemic, a recession, a major infrastructure failure) hits revenue harder than a diversified high street model would.

💾 Strategic divestment is a corporate strategy topic, not just a news story. Swipe for the spec angle.
