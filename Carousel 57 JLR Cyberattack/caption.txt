📌 JLR hit by a cyberattack. Supply chain data exposed. Production disrupted.

📚 Manufacturing businesses are increasingly vulnerable to cyberattacks because their systems are connected: procurement, scheduling, logistics, payments. One breach can stop a production line that never used a computer directly.

📈 GCSE lens (1.5.5 technology): technology enables efficiency but creates single points of failure. A-Level lens (2.4.2 operations management): supply chain disruption has cascading effects on output, delivery and customer trust.

⚠️ The cost to JLR is not just IT recovery. Every day of production loss, every contract penalty, every lost customer order adds up. Business interruption insurance covers some of it.

💾 Cyber risk is now an operations question, not just an IT one. That is your evaluation frame.
