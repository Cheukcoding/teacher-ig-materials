📌 Cocoa passed $10,000 a tonne in 2024, roughly triple its price at the start of 2023. Your chocolate bar got smaller.

📚 Around 60% of the world's cocoa comes from Ivory Coast and Ghana, where harvests failed. Every bar suddenly cost much more to make.

📈 Makers like Mondelez, Cadbury's owner, had two main moves: raise prices, or shrink the bar and keep the price the same. That second one is shrinkflation. GCSE: 1.3.2 costs and profit. A-Level: 1.2.2 supply, with failed harvests shifting supply left.

⚠️ Cocoa prices are volatile. A better harvest could pull that $10,000 back down within a few seasons.
