📌 Crowdfunding. One of the newer sources of finance on the GCSE spec, and one of the most misunderstood in exam answers.

📚 Definition: raising small amounts of money from a large number of people, usually through an online platform, to fund a project or business. Reward-based: backers receive a product or benefit. Equity-based: backers receive a share in the business.

📈 Advantage: no interest payments (reward-based) and no need to impress a bank. The campaign also serves as market research: if no one backs it, there may not be enough demand. Disadvantage: the idea is public. Competitors can see it. And if the campaign fails to reach its target, some platforms return all the money.

⚠️ Crowdfunding works best for consumer products with a clear value proposition. B2B businesses and complex services rarely raise money this way.

💾 The exam question usually asks whether crowdfunding is a suitable source of finance for a specific business. Use the extract to decide.
