📌 AS Business. Ten percent of your marks come from getting definitions right. Most students lose half of those.

📚 On a 4-mark or 8-mark question, the first mark is usually for stating the concept correctly. One mark, every question, every paper. Across a full AS paper, that is a significant proportion of the total.

📈 The definition must be precise. "A break-even is when you make no profit" misses the mechanism. "Break-even is the level of output at which total revenue equals total costs" earns the mark.

⚠️ The trap: writing a vague definition and assuming the analysis will compensate. Examiners can only award marks for what they see written. A K mark is given to the definition, not to the surrounding text.

💾 Learn ten definitions word for word. That is ten guaranteed marks before you write a single chain.
