📌 Consoles get cheaper with age. The Switch 2 just went up £24. And it might not lose many sales.

📚 Switch 2 up 6.1% to £419.99. PS5 up £90. Xbox Series X up £170. Against its substitutes, the Switch 2 became relatively cheaper even as its own price rose. Memory chip costs rose because AI data centres are buying up supply.

📈 GCSE lens (1.4.3): cost rise passed to the customer. A-Level lens (1.2.4 PED): when every substitute gets dearer, demand for your product becomes less elastic. Japan got a 20% rise; the UK got 6.1%. Same product, different elasticities, different price decisions.

⚠️ Nintendo's fastest-selling system, past 23 million units. A business with inelastic demand and no close substitute can price-rise more freely.

💾 PED questions in A-Level papers almost always hide the substitutes in the extract. Find them before you calculate.
