📌 Shein and Temu built a model around a tax exemption. When that changed, so did the pricing.

📚 The de minimis rule meant small parcels under a threshold entered many countries duty-free. Both businesses shipped individual items directly from China to avoid import tax. When the threshold was removed or lowered, the cost advantage shrank.

📈 GCSE lens (1.5.5 technology / 1.5.4 inflation): changes in government regulation shift costs back onto businesses or consumers. A-Level lens (2.5.1 economic influences and trade): import tariffs and tax rules are external factors beyond the business's control.

⚠️ The model still works if volumes are high enough to absorb the new cost, or if consumers value the price enough to accept a small rise. The margin question is the real evaluation.

💾 External factors change competitive advantage overnight. That is why the business environment section always appears in Section B.
