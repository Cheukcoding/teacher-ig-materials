📌 Economies of scale. One of the most cited concepts in A-Level Business. One of the most vaguely explained.

📚 Definition: the reduction in average cost per unit that occurs as a business increases its output. Bigger output spread over fixed costs means each unit costs less to produce.

📈 Types the exam expects: purchasing economies (bulk buying), technical economies (specialist machinery runs cheaper per unit at scale), financial economies (larger businesses borrow at lower rates), managerial economies (specialists replace generalists).

⚠️ The exam does not just ask what economies of scale are. It asks whether this specific business can access them, given its size, sector and current capacity. A small business cannot access technical economies that require £2m machines.

💾 Average cost per unit falls as output rises. That is the mechanism. The type of economy tells you why.

👇 Save this. Economies of scale appear in growth, pricing and operations questions.
