📌 Royal Mail sold to a Czech billionaire. A 500-year-old postal service, now foreign-owned.

📚 EP Group acquired Royal Mail through International Distribution Services. The government approved the deal with conditions on jobs, wages and headquarters location. A national infrastructure asset with a private owner is an ongoing tension.

📈 GCSE lens (1.2.4 competitive environment): privatised monopolies face regulatory control in place of market competition. A-Level lens (3.6 political and legal environment): government conditions on takeovers are a constraint on how the new owner runs the business.

⚠️ Royal Mail delivers to every address in the UK as a Universal Service Obligation. The new owner cannot remove that service. The question is whether it can be made profitable with that constraint.

💾 The Royal Mail story is a privatisation, ownership change and regulatory constraint in one. Three A-Level topics in one case study.
