📌 Stakeholder. The word appears on nearly every A-Level Business question. Most students use it correctly once, then forget who counts.

📚 Definition: any individual or group with an interest in the activities and decisions of a business. Internal stakeholders are inside the business. External stakeholders are outside it.

📈 Shareholders want returns. Employees want pay and security. Customers want value. Suppliers want payment and orders. The community wants jobs and low harm. Government wants tax and compliance. These interests regularly conflict.

⚠️ The exam rarely asks you to list stakeholders. It asks which one matters most in this situation, and why. That requires a judgement based on the extract, not a memorised list.

💾 Stakeholder conflict is an evaluation point. The business that manages it best is your conclusion.

👇 Save this. Stakeholder questions need a hierarchy, not a list.
