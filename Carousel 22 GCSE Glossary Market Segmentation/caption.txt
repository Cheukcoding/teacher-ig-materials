📌 Market segmentation. One of those terms students define correctly and then never actually use.

📚 Definition: dividing a market into groups of customers who share similar needs, wants or characteristics. Demographic, geographic, psychographic, behavioural.

📈 Why it matters on the paper: a business that segments can target its product, price, promotion and place at one group instead of spending on everyone. The exam question usually asks which segment and why, not just what segmentation means.

⚠️ The trap: listing types of segmentation when the question asks about a specific one. Read the extract to find out which segment the business is targeting, then use that.

💾 Segmentation earns marks when you name the group and explain what that group wants, not just the word itself.

👇 Save this. Segmentation comes up on nearly every paper.
