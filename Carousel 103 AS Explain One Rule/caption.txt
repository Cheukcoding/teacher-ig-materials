📌 AS Business. The 4-mark explain question follows one rule that most students break.

📚 One point. Three development steps. No more. Students who write two points in a 4-mark explain question split their development across both and score Level 2 on each. That gives 4 marks at most and usually fewer.

📈 The rule: state the concept (K), apply it to this business using extract detail (A), extend the consequence two more steps (A, A). Every step must cause the next one.

⚠️ AS Business has 4-mark explain questions on Paper 1. The mark scheme for each one allows exactly one chain. Writing two chains means the second one earns nothing unless the first was complete.

💾 One point, three steps, one chain. That is 4/4.

👇 Save this. The rule applies to every 4-mark explain across AS and A-Level.
