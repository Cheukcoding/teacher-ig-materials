📌 Wilko collapsed. 12,500 jobs. 400 stores. Gone in a few weeks.

📚 Wilko had a loyal customer base, a strong brand and 93 years of trading. It also had tight margins, old stock management systems and a debt it could not refinance when lenders lost confidence.

📈 GCSE lens (1.2 growing the business): survival requires matching costs to revenue before cash runs out, not just after. A-Level lens (2.3 financial decisions): a business that cannot raise short-term finance when it needs it faces insolvency regardless of its long-run profitability.

⚠️ Brand loyalty did not save Wilko because brand loyalty cannot pay a supplier in 30 days. Cash flow is not sentiment.

💾 A profitable business can fail. A business with good liquidity survives a bad quarter. The difference matters in every finance question.
