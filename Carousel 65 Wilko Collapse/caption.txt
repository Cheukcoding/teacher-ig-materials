📌 Wilko opened its first store in Leicester in 1930. In 2023 every store closed within ten weeks.

📚 It still had around 400 shops and millions of customers when it entered administration. Over 12,500 jobs went, and no buyer wanted the whole chain.

📈 Popularity doesn't pay the bills. Rent, wages and stock kept rising faster than sales, so cash going out beat cash coming in until borrowing ran out. GCSE: 1.3.3 cash flow. A-Level: 2.3.3 business failure and 2.3.2 liquidity.

⚠️ Ninety-three years of trading ended with The Range paying £5m for the brand and website. None of the shops.
