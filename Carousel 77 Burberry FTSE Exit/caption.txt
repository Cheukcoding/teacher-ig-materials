📌 Burberry dropped out of the FTSE 100. A luxury brand, exited the top tier of UK companies.

📚 A company exits the FTSE 100 when its market capitalisation falls below the threshold. Burberry's market cap fell because its share price fell. The share price fell because investors revised down their expectations of future profit.

📈 GCSE lens (1.2 business ownership): a public limited company's value is set by the market, not by the business itself. A-Level lens (3.2 finance): falling share price reduces the company's ability to raise equity finance at good terms.

⚠️ Being in or out of the FTSE 100 is not itself a business problem. The underlying cause, falling demand in key luxury markets, is.

💾 FTSE 100 membership is a consequence of performance, not a cause of it. The evaluation point is always the underlying business condition.
