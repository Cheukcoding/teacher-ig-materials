📌 Burberry has been around since 1856. In September 2024 it dropped out of the FTSE 100.

📚 Comparable store sales fell around 20% in the quarter to June 2024. By July, the chief executive had been replaced.

📈 Burberry pushed its prices up to sit with the top fashion houses. Its loyal customers found them too high, and top-tier luxury buyers didn't see it as exclusive enough. It lost its place on the market map from both sides. GCSE: 1.2.3 market mapping. A-Level: 1.1.3 market positioning and 1.2.5 income elasticity.

⚠️ Luxury spending slowed everywhere, especially in China, so not all of that 20% fall was Burberry's own doing. The new boss is going back to the trench coat.
