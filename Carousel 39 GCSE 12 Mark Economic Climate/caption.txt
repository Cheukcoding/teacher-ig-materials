📌 12-mark question on the economic climate. The most common mistake: writing about every economic factor instead of the one in the question.

📚 The economic climate question usually gives a specific context: recession, inflation, rising interest rates. The answer must be about that specific pressure on that specific business.

📈 Structure: two developed points on how this economic factor affects this business, followed by a conclusion that says which effect matters most and why. The conclusion adds a new reason linked to the context.

⚠️ Recession affects a budget supermarket differently from a luxury brand. Inflation affects a business with long supplier contracts differently from one buying spot. Read the extract before you decide which effect to argue.

💾 The 12-mark economic climate question is almost always in Section B. The extract tells you which factor to focus on. Use that, not your general knowledge of the economy.
