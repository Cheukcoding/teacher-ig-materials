📌 Inditex, Zara's parent company, posted strong profits. Fast fashion's most profitable operator kept growing while others struggled.

📚 Inditex's model: design to shelf in two to three weeks. Most fast fashion takes months. This means Inditex responds to real demand instead of forecasting it. Less unsold stock. Less markdown. Better margin.

📈 GCSE lens (2.2.4 product / 2.4.2 operations): a short production cycle reduces inventory risk and allows rapid response to trends. A-Level lens (2.4.3 lean production): pull-based production responds to demand rather than pushing stock into stores and discounting it later.

⚠️ The Inditex model requires tight control of the supply chain and significant investment in logistics. Competitors cannot copy the speed without also copying the infrastructure, which costs billions and takes years.
