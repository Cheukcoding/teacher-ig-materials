📌 BTEC Business Units 1, 2 and 3. A different exam. A different skill set.

📚 BTEC Unit 1 is a controlled assessment on business environments. Unit 2 is a set assignment on finance and human resources. Unit 3 is a synoptic exam. Each one tests application to a real business context, not just knowledge of terms.

📈 BTEC students often know the content well. The gap is in structuring the controlled assessment response to the brief, and in the Unit 3 synoptic exam where questions use unfamiliar contexts.

⚠️ BTEC and A-Level Business share content but not exam technique. They need separate approaches, especially on Unit 3 where the question style differs from Edexcel A-Level.
