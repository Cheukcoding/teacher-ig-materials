📌 BYD outsold Tesla globally in electric vehicles. The market leader changed.

📚 BYD grew by expanding into lower price points and new markets. Tesla held a premium position. When a challenger undercuts on price and widens the range, volume shifts, even if the premium brand keeps its margin.

📈 GCSE lens (1.2.4 competition): new entrants and lower-cost rivals can take market share from an established leader. A-Level lens (1.3.3 pricing): competitive pricing by BYD forced the entire EV market to reconsider its price points.

⚠️ Market share by volume and market share by revenue can tell different stories. BYD sold more units. Tesla still earns more per car.

💾 Every market leader looked safe until it didn't. That is your evaluation point.
