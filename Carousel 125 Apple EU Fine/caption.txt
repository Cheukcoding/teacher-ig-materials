📌 In March 2024 the European Commission fined Apple €1.8bn over how it treated music streaming apps.

📚 The investigation started with a complaint from Spotify. At the time, the App Store was the only way to install apps on an iPhone in Europe.

📈 Apple blocked music apps from telling iPhone users they could pay less outside the App Store. The Commission ruled that an abuse of a dominant position. Apple runs the store and competes with the apps in it. GCSE: 1.5.3 legislation. A-Level: 2.5.2 competition law and 4.4.3 controlling MNCs.

⚠️ Apple said it would appeal the €1.8bn. With tougher digital rules in Europe, more cases may follow.
