📌 The EU fined Apple billions. The App Store, the payment system, the browser defaults. Regulators decided Apple used its market position to restrict competition.

📚 Apple's ecosystem is designed so that every device, app, payment and subscription flows through Apple's own infrastructure. That creates lock-in for users and lock-out for competitors. The EU's Digital Markets Act is designed to break that up.

📈 GCSE lens (1.5.4 government influence / 1.5.5 technology): governments regulate technology companies when their market power reduces consumer choice. A-Level lens (3.6 political and legal): competition law fines are a cost of market dominance, not just a legal inconvenience.

⚠️ Apple's response: minimal compliance, maximum delay, legal challenge at every step. The fine is large but Apple's cash reserves are larger. The question is whether regulatory pressure eventually changes the business model or just the fine amount.

💾 Tech regulation is a built-in evaluation topic: regulation limits profit now versus competition costs more later.
