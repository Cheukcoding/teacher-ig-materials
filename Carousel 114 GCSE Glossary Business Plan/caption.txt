📌 Business plan. The term every GCSE student defines. The question is always about what it does, not what it contains.

📚 Definition: a written document outlining a business's goals, strategies, financial forecasts and operational plans. It is used to secure finance, guide decisions and measure progress.

📈 Why lenders and investors want one: it shows the entrepreneur has thought through the risks, tested the assumptions and has a plan to generate enough revenue to repay any borrowing.

⚠️ A business plan is not a guarantee of success. It is a planning tool. The exam question often asks what happens if one section of the plan turns out to be wrong: the revenue forecast, the cost estimate, the market size assumption.

💾 The most commonly tested section in GCSE questions: the financial forecast. Revenue, costs, break-even, cash flow. Know how each one connects to the plan.
