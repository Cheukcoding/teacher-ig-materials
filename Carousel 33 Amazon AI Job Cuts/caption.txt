📌 Amazon cut thousands of roles, pointing to AI. This is not new. But this time, it is reaching office jobs.

📚 AI is replacing tasks, not whole jobs, for now. Admin, analysis, customer contact, code review. Roles built on repetitive processing are most at risk. Roles built on judgement, relationships and creative work are less so.

📈 GCSE lens (1.5.5 technology): automation changes labour demand. Businesses that reduce headcount save on fixed costs and can offer lower prices or hold higher margins. A-Level lens (2.4.4 workforce planning): fewer employees means different skill requirements, redundancy costs and retraining decisions.

⚠️ The productivity gain is real. The question is who benefits. Shareholders gain from lower costs. Workers in displaced roles bear the transition cost.

💾 Technology decisions are always also stakeholder decisions. That is your evaluation.
