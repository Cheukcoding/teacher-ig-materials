📌 AS Business. Four calculation questions use the same formula in reverse. Most students only practise it one way.

📚 Break-even, contribution, gross profit margin, and mark-up all appear as standard calculations and as reverse calculations. The question gives you the answer and asks for one of the inputs.

📈 Example: break-even is 4,000 units, contribution is £5. What are the fixed costs? Fixed costs = break-even x contribution = £20,000. Most students who know the formula forwards cannot reverse it under pressure.

⚠️ The reverse calculation is not harder. It just requires you to rearrange the formula before you plug in the numbers. Practise both directions for every formula on the spec.

💾 For every formula: practise solving for each variable in turn. That is three versions of the same formula instead of one.

👇 Save this. Reverse calculations appear on almost every AS paper.
