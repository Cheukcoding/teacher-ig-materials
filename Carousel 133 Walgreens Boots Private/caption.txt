📌 Walgreens is taking Boots private. The high street pharmacy chain, listed publicly since 1997, going back into private hands.

📚 A business goes private when the owners believe the market is undervaluing it and they can grow better without quarterly reporting pressure. Private ownership allows longer-term investment decisions without shareholder scrutiny every ninety days.

📈 GCSE lens (1.2 business ownership): moving from public limited company to private ownership removes the ability to raise equity from the public but also removes the obligation to publish detailed financials and hold AGMs. A-Level lens (3.2 finance): private equity ownership typically demands high returns in a shorter time frame, often through cost cuts, debt-loading and eventual resale.

⚠️ Going private is not the same as going out of business. Boots will still trade. The ownership structure changes, not the shops.

💾 Ownership structure changes are built-in evaluation topics. The advantages of each structure are also the disadvantages of the alternative.
