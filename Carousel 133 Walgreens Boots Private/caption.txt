📌 Boots was founded in Nottingham in 1849. In 2025 its owner, Walgreens Boots Alliance, agreed to be bought and taken off the stock market.

📚 Private equity firm Sycamore Partners agreed a deal worth around $10bn. Walgreens had been struggling with falling profits in its US pharmacies.

📈 Public shareholders expect steady results every quarter. A private owner can make slower, bigger changes out of public view, possibly including separating Boots. GCSE: 1.4.1 ownership. A-Level: 1.5.4 forms of business and 3.2.2 takeovers.

⚠️ Private equity aims to improve a business and later sell it. Staff and customers face changes decided by a new owner.
