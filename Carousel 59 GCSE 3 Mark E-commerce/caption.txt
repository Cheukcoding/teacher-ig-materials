📌 3-mark question on e-commerce. The answer is one point with two linked steps. Most students write a definition and stop.

📚 E-commerce is selling goods or services online. That is K. The marks come from what e-commerce does for this specific business in this extract.

📈 Example chain: "One benefit of e-commerce is that the business can sell to customers nationally. This is because the website removes the need for a physical location, leading to higher potential sales volume and lower costs per unit sold."

⚠️ The spec point is 1.5.5 technology. The question will usually give a specific business context: a small local retailer considering going online, or a business that has just launched a website.

💾 Three-mark questions reward depth, not breadth. One point, two steps, one chain. Nothing else.
