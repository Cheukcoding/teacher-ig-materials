📌 Limited liability. The exam question is almost never just "what does it mean." It is "why does it matter to this business."

📚 Definition: the financial responsibility of an owner is limited to the amount they invested. Personal assets are protected. The business is a separate legal entity from the person who owns it.

📈 Why it matters: with limited liability, investors are more willing to put money in, because the worst that happens is they lose their investment. Without it, a failing business can take the owner's house, savings and car.

⚠️ The downside the exam asks about: sole traders do not have limited liability. A partnership usually does not either. Only a private or public limited company provides it automatically.

💾 The word "limited" in a company name is a legal statement, not a description of the business size.
