📌 10-mark assess question using Timpson. The extract gives you the business model. Most students ignore it and write about Timpson from memory.

📚 Timpson's model: highly devolved management, employees given wide decision-making freedom, recruited partly from prison leavers. The question will test whether this model suits a specific strategic decision.

📈 10-mark assess: two points, one on each side, three development steps each. The examiner marks the quality of the chain, not the number of points. One well-developed side scores higher than three thin ones.

⚠️ Application in a 10-mark question means linking to specific details in the extract, not just naming the business. "Timpson" without the detail of its model is not application.

💾 Every A-Level case study business has a few key facts in the extract. Use those. They are there because the examiner put them there for a reason.
