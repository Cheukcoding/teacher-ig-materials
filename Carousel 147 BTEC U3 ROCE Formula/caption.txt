📌 BTEC Unit 3. Return on Capital Employed. One of the financial calculations that appears most often in the synoptic exam.

📚 ROCE = (operating profit / capital employed) x 100. Capital employed = total assets - current liabilities. A higher ROCE means the business generates more profit per pound of capital invested.

📈 In Unit 3 evaluation: a ROCE that is higher than the current interest rate means the business is generating more from its capital than it could by leaving the money in a bank. A ROCE below the interest rate suggests the investment is not covering its opportunity cost.

⚠️ BTEC Unit 3 gives you data across two or three years. Calculate ROCE for each year, identify the trend, and link the trend to the case study context. A calculation without a trend analysis scores fewer marks.
