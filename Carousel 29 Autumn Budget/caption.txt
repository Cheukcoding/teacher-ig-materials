📌 The Autumn Budget lands every year. And every year, it becomes a Section B extract.

📚 Budgets change tax rates, minimum wages, public spending and borrowing. Each of those changes costs something for businesses. A rise in employer National Insurance goes straight to labour costs. A rise in minimum wage does the same.

📈 GCSE lens (1.5.4 inflation / 2.1 business finance): cost increases squeeze margins, especially for small businesses with fixed prices and no power over suppliers. A-Level lens (2.5.1 economic influences): fiscal policy affects consumer spending, business investment and borrowing costs together.

⚠️ Budget headlines and business reality are different things. A tax cut for consumers boosts demand only if they spend the saving. An investment allowance only helps a business that was already planning to invest.

💾 Budget announcements are ready-made evaluation: what the government intended versus what businesses actually experience.
