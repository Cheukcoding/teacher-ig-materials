📌 Same GCSE 2025 question, same length. One script got 4/9, the other got 9/9.

📚 The word that split them: Justify. That means argue ONE option both ways, then decide. Covering both options' upsides doesn't count.

📈 The 9/9 script picked "raise prices," gave one advantage, then argued against its own pick (loyal customers upset by the rise), then named why it rejected the other option.

⚠️ The 4/9 script only argued the upside. No counter-argument to your own choice, no evaluation marks, however well written.

💾 One extra paragraph, arguing against yourself, is worth 5 extra marks.
