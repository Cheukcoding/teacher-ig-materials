📌 Globalisation. One of the wider context topics that connects to almost every other topic on the GCSE spec.

📚 Definition: the process by which businesses, people and governments around the world become increasingly connected and interdependent. Trade, investment, technology and migration all flow more freely between countries.

📈 For business: globalisation opens new markets (export) and new sources of supply (cheaper labour or materials). It also increases competition from foreign rivals and exposes businesses to currency risk and supply chain disruption.

⚠️ The exam trap: treating globalisation as uniformly positive. For a small UK manufacturer, foreign competition can reduce domestic sales. The evaluation depends on whether the business exports or competes with imports.

💾 Globalisation is most likely to appear in the external environment section or in questions about market entry and competition.

👇 Save this. Globalisation questions appear on every GCSE and A-Level paper.
