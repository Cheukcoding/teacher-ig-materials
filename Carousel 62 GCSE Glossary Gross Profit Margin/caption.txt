📌 Gross profit margin. One of three profitability ratios GCSE expects you to calculate and interpret.

📚 Formula: (gross profit / revenue) x 100. Gross profit = revenue - cost of goods sold. Gross profit margin shows how much of each pound of revenue is left after paying for the direct costs of making the product.

📈 A falling gross profit margin means costs of production are rising faster than prices. A rising one means the business is either selling at a higher price or reducing its direct costs.

⚠️ Gross profit margin and net profit margin measure different things. Gross ignores overheads. Net deducts them. A business with a high gross margin but a low net margin has high overhead costs eating into its profit.

💾 The examiner will give you two years of data. Calculate both margins, compare them, and explain what changed.
