📌 One price for Clubcard holders, a higher price for everyone else. The Competition and Markets Authority looked into it.

📚 Two-tier pricing is not new. But making the higher price the "normal" price, and the lower price the "member" price, raises questions about what the real price actually is. Is the Clubcard price a discount, or is the shelf price a premium?

📈 GCSE lens (2.2.3 pricing): psychological pricing uses reference points to make a deal feel better than it is. A-Level lens (1.3.3): price discrimination separates markets by membership to extract more from non-members.

⚠️ The CMA's argument: if the "original" price was never genuinely sold at, it is misleading to present a lower price as a saving from it.

💾 Pricing strategy is never just a number. It is a signal, a legal position, and a competitive choice.
