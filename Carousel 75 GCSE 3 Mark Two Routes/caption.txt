📌 3-mark explain question with two possible answers. Pick one. Go deep. Do not cover both.

📚 Some students see two routes in a question and write a half-paragraph on each. Both paragraphs are incomplete. Both score 1 mark. Together they give 2/3 at most, usually 1.

📈 Full chain for one route: "One way is X. This is because Y, leading to Z." Three steps, one route, 3 marks. Two half-chains: "One way is X. Another way is Y." Two marks at best.

⚠️ This is the most common way students lose marks on 3-mark questions. It is a technique error, not a content error. The content knowledge is fine. The choice of depth over breadth is not.

💾 Three-mark questions: one point, three steps. Nothing else needed.

👇 Save this. It applies to every 3-marker on the paper.
